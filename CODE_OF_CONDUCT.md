# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in BloomOS welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Accept feedback gracefully and prioritize user safety.
- Respect privacy, licenses, and attribution.

Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove or reject contributions and restrict participation when behavior violates these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method published by the project repository. Until that contact is configured, do not include sensitive details in a public issue.

This policy is intentionally concise for the bootstrap phase and may be replaced by a formally adopted community standard before the first public release.
